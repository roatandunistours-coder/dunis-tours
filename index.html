A few common questions

Where do you pick us up?

We pick you up at Isla Tropicale (formerly Mahogany Bay), the Port of Roatán in Coxen Hole, the airport, or your hotel. Tell us your ship or hotel when you write, and we’ll send meeting details.

Can you just drop us at West Bay Beach?

Yes. If you’d rather explore West Bay Beach on your own, a beach drop-off is $50 per person.

What if our ship doesn’t dock?

A 30% deposit holds the tour, and it is fully refundable if the ship doesn’t dock.

Can we hold the sloths or monkeys?

Sloths and monkeys can be watched and photographed. We do not offer holding.

How do we pay?

A 30% deposit holds the tour, by PayPal, Zelle or Venmo. The rest can be paid any way you like.

Are tips included?

Tips for Dunis or your driver aren’t included, but they’re always appreciated if you had a great day. That’s separate from any tip you leave at the restaurant at West Bay Beach, which is paid directly to the restaurant.

Are food and drinks included?

Food and drinks at West Bay Beach are served right to your spot and paid directly to the restaurant, along with any tip you’d like to leave there. That’s separate from any tip for Dunis or your driver.

Are there kids’ prices?

Kids 5 and under are free. Ages 6 and up are the regular $70.

Can kids do the ATV tour?

Little ones can ride along with a parent. Just ask us and we’ll set it up.

How long is the tour?

Tours are timed to your ship’s schedule, typically about 4–5 hours. We plan around all-aboard so you’re back with time to spare.
