Questions guests ask

Can you take a family or a group of six or more?

Yes. Your group rides together privately. Dunis arranges the right van for your headcount. Groups of 8 or more save $5 each. Kids 0–5 ride free and do not count toward the group size.

What to bring

A swimsuit, a towel, sunscreen, and cash for food and drinks at the beach. Water shoes help if you snorkel.

How do I hold the tour?

A $25 deposit per paying guest holds the tour. The deposit is refunded in full if the ship does not dock. Resort guests get a full refund if they cancel 48 hours ahead. Cash is fine on tour day for the balance.

Where do you pick us up?

Pickup is at Isla Tropicale (formerly Mahogany Bay), the Port of Roatán in Coxen Hole, the airport, or your hotel.

How does pricing work?

Kids 0–5 are free and do not count toward group size. One paying guest pays the 2-guest total. Two or three paying guests pay $20 more each. Four to seven guests pay the menu price. Groups of 8 or more save $5 each.

Are tips included?

Tips are welcome if you had a great tour. You can thank Dunis directly.

Ready to hold the tour?

Message Dunis with your tour, your headcount, and your ship or hotel. A $25 deposit per paying guest holds the tour.
