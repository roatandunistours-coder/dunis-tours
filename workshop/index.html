Our Story

DUNIS RUNS THE DAY

Dunis has driven Roatán cruise days for 21 years. Fluent English. He is usually in the seat. When two ships hit at once, local drivers who know both docks and the same 90-minute return take the extra vans. Mayan Eden, chocolate, rum, then West Bay — back before all-aboard.

  • Built for cruise days

    4–6 hours, or as long as the ship allows. We watch all-aboard, not a clock on a brochure.

  • Private vehicle

    Car for 1–2 people. Van for families and groups. A/C. Your group only.

  • Dunis first

    21 years with cruisers. Overflow is local and English-speaking — not a random taxi.
